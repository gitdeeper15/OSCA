## Conceptual and Methodological Foundations

The research builds on six intersecting domains:

### 1. Computer Science and Software Engineering
- Self-modifying code and runtime code rewriting.
- Engineered self-operating systems.
- Governance of autonomous agents.

### 2. Applied Mathematics and Numerical Computing
- Numerical analysis and numerical linear algebra.
- Operator theory and Riemannian geometry.
- Dynamical equilibrium theory.

### 3. Cybersecurity and Systems Theory
- Cryptographic verification and digital proof.
- Security of autonomous systems.
- Digital evidence integrity.
- Multi-tier control and governance theory.

### 4. Computational Physics
- Physical noise modeling.
- Statistical modeling.
- Empirical verification of physical systems.

### 5. Quantum Computing
- Quantum processor architecture.
- Photonic quantum computing.
- Superconducting processors.
- Testing on real quantum hardware.

### 6. Artificial Intelligence and Autonomous Systems
- Autonomous agents.
- Semantic governance.
- Action-space control.
- Execution and decision reliability.
